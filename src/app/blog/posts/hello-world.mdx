Hi, I'm Devanshu 👋

I'm starting this blog to think out loud about data science and share the things I build.
Expect short, practical write-ups rather than long theory dumps.

## What I'll write about

- **Project build logs** — how I designed, trained, and shipped things like NeuroRAG and the
  overspeed-detection system, including the parts that didn't work the first time.
- **Machine learning & AI** — notes on models, RAG, and turning experiments into something usable.
- **Data & analytics** — practical tips on wrangling messy data and communicating results.

## Why write at all?

Writing forces me to actually understand what I did. If a post helps one other person avoid a
rabbit hole I fell into, that's a win.

More soon — thanks for reading.
