## Overview

A deep-learning project that forecasts stock prices from historical equity
time-series data, framed around predicting the *direction* of the next move
rather than just an absolute price.

## Problem

Stock prices are noisy and non-stationary — their statistical properties drift
over time, which breaks naive models and makes honest evaluation tricky (it's
easy to leak future information into the past).

## Approach

- Built an **LSTM-based** forecasting model on historical equity data
- Handled the non-stationarity of the price series
- Used **sliding-window** sequence generation with train/test splits that
  respect temporal order — no look-ahead leakage
- Packaged the whole thing as a modular Python pipeline

## Results

Achieved **88.60% directional accuracy** on held-out data — correctly calling
the direction of the next price movement.
