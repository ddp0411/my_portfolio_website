## Overview

A real-time computer-vision system that detects vehicles in traffic footage,
reads their number plates, and estimates their speed — flagging vehicles that
exceed the limit. This work is backed by two published research papers.

## Problem

Manual speed enforcement doesn't scale, and estimating speed reliably from
ordinary video is hard: vehicles change size with perspective, occlude one
another, and move fast between frames.

## Approach

- Vehicle detection with a **YOLOv8** (You Only Look Once) model
- **Optical Character Recognition (OCR)** to read number plates
- **Dual-camera analytics** to estimate speed from the time and distance between
  camera crossings
- A **Streamlit** front-end for an interactive GUI

## Results

Published in two peer-reviewed papers — see the [Published Research](/work/publications) entry.
