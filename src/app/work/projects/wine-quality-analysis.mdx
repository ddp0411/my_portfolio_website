## Overview

A classification project that predicts the quality rating of wine from its
measurable physicochemical properties — acidity, residual sugar, alcohol, and
more.

## Problem

Quality scoring by human tasters is subjective and expensive. Can objective
chemical measurements predict how a wine will be rated?

## Approach

- Explored the physicochemical features and their relationship to quality
- Trained a **Random Forest** classifier
- Tuned hyperparameters to maximise accuracy

## Results

Achieved **92.18% accuracy** after hyperparameter tuning.
